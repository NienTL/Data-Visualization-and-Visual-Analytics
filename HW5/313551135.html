Some Notes

  • Use the Menu to sort the data by the attributes, and select "Ascending" or "Descending."
  • You can lighten the legend by clicking on it to show the specific criteria. Also, click again to restore.
  • You are free to move your mouse over each bar to show its attribute and value.
  • Please note: if you choose a legend, you can only see the value of the chosen criteria by hovering your mouse.
    This is to let the data be clearly showed.
